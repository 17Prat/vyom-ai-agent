# Instagram Integration Guide
## Project: Brahmand
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Instagram is Brahmand's primary social storytelling channel.
Treat Instagram as a premium brand communication platform rather than a casual social media account.
Everything generated should reinforce Brahmand's identity as:
- Modern
- Spiritual
- Premium
- Timeless
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Beautiful

Never produce content that feels clickbait, loud, or overly commercial.

---

## Brand Voice
- **Calm.**
- **Minimal.**
- **Thought-provoking.**
- **Elegant.**
- **Sacred.**
- **Every caption should feel like a small meditation.**

---

## Writing Style
**Use:**
- Short paragraphs
- Strong opening sentence
- White space
- Elegant typography
- Emotional storytelling

**Avoid (DO NOT DO THIS):**
- ❌ Excess emojis
- ❌ Clickbait
- ❌ Fake urgency
- ❌ Cheap motivational quotes
- ❌ Generic AI language

---

## Color Palette & Visuals
**Colors:**
- Primary Background: Deep Space Black (#05070A)
- Primary Accent: Sacred Gold (#D4AF37)
- Secondary: Warm Ivory (#F7F3EC)
- Highlights: Cosmic Blue (#2C5EFF)

**Typography:** Elegant Serif + Modern Sans Serif (Minimal hierarchy, lots of whitespace).

**Visual Identity Requirements:**
- Instagram should feel like: **Apple meets NASA meets Ancient Indian Wisdom**.
- Never use loud gradients, oversaturated orange, or generic AI artwork.
- **Preferred imagery:** Temples, Sacred geometry, Cosmos, Stars, Ancient scriptures, Soft light, Stone textures, Gold details.
- **Image Style:** Cinematic, Dark, Premium, Museum quality, Soft lighting, Golden highlights, Natural textures. No stock-photo feeling.

---

## Content Pillars
1. **Daily Dharma:** Short wisdom.
2. **Temples of India:** History, Architecture, Meaning.
3. **Festivals:** Educational, Beautiful.
4. **Jaap:** Mantras, Pronunciation, Meaning.
5. **Community Stories:** Real people.
6. **Bharat Heritage:** Art, Culture, History.
7. **Brahmand Updates:** App announcements, Features, Launches.

---

## Output Formats

### Caption Structure
1. Hook
2. Story
3. Meaning
4. Reflection
5. Soft CTA (e.g., "Explore more inside Brahmand.")
- *Never say:* "Like, Share, Subscribe."

### Hashtags
- Use only relevant hashtags. (Maximum: 8)
- Preferred: `#BrahmandApp #Sanatan #Temple #Dharma #IndianCulture #SpiritualJourney`

### Reels
- Slow pacing.
- Ambient music.
- Minimal text.
- Elegant transitions.
- Educational.

### Carousels
- Large typography.
- One idea per slide.
- Minimal text.
- Strong visual hierarchy.

---

## Strict AI Rules (Forbidden Items)
- **Never** invent historical facts.
- **Never** invent Sanskrit verses.
- **Always** verify religious information. When uncertain, explicitly say you are uncertain.
- **Forbidden Content:** Political content, Religious hate, Fear-based marketing, Cheap marketing tactics, Clickbait, Spam, Fake engagement.

## Ultimate Goal
Every Instagram post should make someone feel:
**"I learned something beautiful today."**
Not:
*"I saw another social media post."*
